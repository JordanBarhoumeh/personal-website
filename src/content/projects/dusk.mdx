## Overview

Dusk is an iOS app that gives you a daily summary of your sleep, your movement and the shape of your day, all in one place. It reads from the apps and sensors you already use and brings them together as a set of simple summary cards.

## What it shows

The summary is built from three sources on your phone:

- **Health data**, through HealthKit: sleep stages and duration, step count, walking distance, active minutes and resting heart rate.
- **Your calendar**, through EventKit: today's and upcoming events, used to build the Your Day card.
- **Your approximate location**, through CoreLocation: used only to add local weather context.

## Private by design

Dusk processes everything on the device. Health, calendar and location data are never uploaded to a server, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or profiling. A local cache of daily summaries stays in the app's private container and is removed when the app is deleted.

The app works with whatever permissions you grant. Some cards show less data if you skip a permission, but nothing breaks.

## Premium

Dusk offers an optional premium subscription. Purchases and cancellations are handled by Apple through the App Store, so the app never collects or stores payment information.
